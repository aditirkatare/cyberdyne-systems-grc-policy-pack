# GRC Audit Test Plan

This is a design-stage test plan. No tests have been executed against a real Cyberdyne Systems environment. When used operationally, record tester, date, sample population, evidence link, result, finding ID, and reviewer for every test.

## Result definitions

- **Pass:** the control objective is met for the defined population/sample and sufficient evidence supports the conclusion.
- **Fail:** a required control is absent, not performed, or ineffective for one or more tested items.
- **Partial:** a control exists but coverage, evidence, consistency, or timeliness is incomplete.
- **Not tested:** population or evidence was unavailable; do not treat this as a pass.

## Test procedures

| Test ID | Area / policy | Objective and population | Procedure / sample | Pass criteria | Failure follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AT-01 | MFA / Identity Standard | Verify active workforce accounts in Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, and AWS are protected by MFA. | Obtain full active-user exports per platform; reconcile counts to identity inventory. Inspect all privileged accounts and a sample of 10 standard accounts or the entire population if smaller. | Every active in-scope account has MFA or a current, approved exception with compensating controls and expiry. Privileged accounts use phishing-resistant MFA where supported or have documented remediation. | Open a finding, remediate immediately for privileged gaps, assign owner and due date, retest. |
| AT-02 | Password management / Identity Standard | Verify approved password-manager use and absence of shared-password practices. | Inspect approved tooling and onboarding records; sample 10 users or all if fewer; check configuration guidance and user acknowledgement. Do not collect or view users' actual passwords. | The approved manager is available, training/instructions exist, and sampled users confirm unique business passwords are stored there. No plaintext password evidence is present in sampled approved work locations. | Provide training/configuration fix; review exposed secrets; record a finding if unsafe storage is confirmed. |
| AT-03 | Joiners / Access Control | Verify access grants have business approval and least-privilege scope. | Sample 5 joiners from the review period or all if fewer. Trace each access assignment to a request, approver, role, and provisioning record. | All sampled grants have approval before provisioning, correct role/group assignment, and required baseline controls. | Remove excess access, correct the workflow, and expand sample if exceptions indicate systematic issues. |
| AT-04 | Movers / Access Control | Verify obsolete permissions are removed after role changes. | Sample 5 role changes or all if fewer. Compare access before and after the change with manager request and system records. | Unneeded former-role access is removed or has documented justification and expiry. | Correct access and review similar role-change cases. |
| AT-05 | Leavers / Access Control | Verify access revocation occurs by the defined target and all credentials are considered. | Sample all leavers in the period when population is 10 or fewer; otherwise sample 10 including any involuntary/high-risk departures if present. Compare HR effective time with IdP, app, cloud, repository, token, device-return, and ownership-transfer records. | High-risk/involuntary access is disabled at effective time where synchronized process exists; standard access is disabled within one hour; exceptions are evidenced and escalated. Tokens, keys, sessions, devices, and ownership are addressed. | Treat late high-risk revocation as high-priority finding; validate root cause and retest next leaver event. |
| AT-06 | Access reviews / Access Control | Verify periodic privileged and standard access reviews are complete and actionable. | Obtain complete user/role population and latest review records. Inspect 100% of privileged roles and sample 10 standard access decisions or the full population if smaller. | Reviews meet cadence; reviewer is appropriate; decisions are recorded; revocations/modifications have closure evidence; full population is accounted for. | Re-run review with accountable owners and track missed actions. |
| AT-07 | Data classification / Data Policy | Verify high-value information has an owner and classification. | Select 10 representative datasets across customer, source-code, workforce, and operational data; inspect inventory, owner, label, location, and approved access. | All sampled datasets have an owner, sensible classification, approved storage, and access consistent with classification. | Assign owner/classification, restrict exposed data immediately, and broaden review for systemic gaps. |
| AT-08 | External sharing / Data Policy | Verify external sharing of Confidential/Restricted data is authorized and scoped. | Review available sharing logs and sample 10 external shares or all if fewer. Inspect recipient, business purpose, owner approval, permissions, expiry, and deletion where applicable. | Each tested share has an appropriate owner approval, verified recipient, least-privilege settings, and expiry/retention decision. | Revoke unapproved access, assess incident-reporting obligations, and identify root cause. |
| AT-09 | Incident intake / Incident Policy | Verify suspected incidents have a usable reporting path and records distinguish fact from hypothesis. | Conduct a tabletop or dry-run report; inspect a sample of incidents/near misses where available. | The report reaches the designated responder; a unique record captures key timestamps, severity, owner, evidence location, actions, and communications. No secret is pasted into the general channel. | Fix routing/contact failures; repeat dry-run; assign missing incident-record fields. |
| AT-10 | Logging and evidence / Incident Policy | Verify expected security-relevant log sources are identified and accessible to authorized responders. | Compare a source inventory with provider settings for sign-ins, MFA changes, privileged changes, GitHub, AWS, and endpoint alerts. Inspect a sample of recent events and retention settings. | Relevant events are enabled where supported, retained for the approved period, searchable by responders, and access to logs is restricted. | Document coverage gaps and risk; prioritize high-impact missing logs and test after remediation. |
| AT-11 | Exceptions / Governance | Verify exceptions are authorized, time-bound, and reviewed. | Inspect the full exception register. | Every active exception has scope, business reason, risk, compensating controls, named owner, approver, expiry, and review status. Expired exceptions are closed or re-approved. | Escalate expired/high-risk exceptions; disable access or apply compensating controls where appropriate. |
| AT-12 | Endpoint baseline / AUP | Verify managed laptops have core protections enabled. | Compare endpoint inventory to MDM/EDR, disk encryption, screen-lock, and patch-status exports; sample 10 devices or all if fewer. | All sampled devices meet baseline or have an approved, time-bound exception; inventory and security-tool populations reconcile. | Isolate or remediate unprotected high-risk devices; track missing inventory and exceptions. |

## Finding record fields

Finding ID; related requirement; risk statement; test ID; population and sample; evidence references; result; severity; root cause; affected assets/data; compensating controls; owner; remediation plan; due date; risk acceptance if applicable; retest result/date; closure approver.

## Test governance

- Test only systems and information for which the tester is authorized.
- Use exports and redacted evidence where practical; never include passwords, access tokens, private keys, or unnecessary personal data in the report.
- Record sampling limitations, population completeness, and unavailable evidence. A sample pass does not prove every control worked for every event.
- Link findings to the risk register and remediation tracker. Management acceptance must be explicit, time-bound, and assigned to a person with appropriate authority.
