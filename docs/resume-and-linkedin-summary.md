## Interview talk track

**Problem:** A small SaaS company needs consistent rules for identity, access lifecycle, sensitive data, employee device use, and security incident response, but does not yet have a mature security-governance baseline.

**Approach:** Define practical policies around the assumed tech stack; assign owners; map the intent to NIST CSF 2.0 and CIS Controls v8.1 at a high level; specify evidence and review cadence; design repeatable tests; and sequence implementation over 30/60/90 days.

**Trade-off I made:** Kept the scope to five core policies rather than claiming to build an entire ISMS. I left backup/recovery as an explicit risk gap and labelled residual risk as a target rather than evidence-based, demonstrating that policy documents alone do not prove controls operate effectively.

**Next improvements:** Add Secure SDLC/Change Management, Backup & Recovery, Vulnerability Management, and Third-Party Risk Management standards; validate cloud configuration requirements; create an actual sample evidence set from a sandbox environment; and run a tabletop plus control test in that sandbox.
