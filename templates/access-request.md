## Reviewer checks

- [ ] Requester cannot approve their own access.
- [ ] Named human account or documented service identity is used.
- [ ] Minimum required permissions are requested.
- [ ] MFA requirements are met or an approved exception exists.
- [ ] Production/admin access has the required additional approval.
- [ ] Expiry/review date is present when access is temporary or elevated.
- [ ] Request, approval, and provisioning evidence are linked.
