## Approval checks

- [ ] The requirement being excepted is specific.
- [ ] Legal, contractual, privacy, and customer obligations have been checked.
- [ ] Scope and affected users/systems/data are clear.
- [ ] Compensating controls reduce risk and have an owner.
- [ ] Expiry and review dates are set.
- [ ] Risk is accepted by an authorized person, not just the requester.
- [ ] Evidence is stored in the restricted GRC location.
