## Population completeness checks

- [ ] Export covers the full system/user/group population for the review date.
- [ ] Privileged roles, direct grants, guest/vendor accounts, service identities, tokens/keys, and break-glass accounts are in scope or explicitly documented as separate reviews.
- [ ] Every account has a business owner or is investigated as an orphaned identity.
- [ ] Reviewer is independent of the account being reviewed where practical.
- [ ] All revocations/changes have closure evidence.
- [ ] Exceptions have valid approver, compensating controls, expiry, and review date.
- [ ] Review record is stored in the restricted evidence location.
